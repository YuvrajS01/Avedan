# Product Copy Guidelines

## Tone

- clear
- reassuring
- practical
- non-technical by default
- concise

## Preferred phrases

- `Make it fit`
- `Prepare your photo`
- `Prepare your signature`
- `Technical checks passed`
- `Your image stays on this device`
- `Within the required file-size range`

## Avoid

- `100% guaranteed acceptance`
- `Officially approved`
- exaggerated AI claims
- unexplained jargon such as `JPEG quantization` in primary UI

## Requirement disclaimer

Where form presets are used, communicate that requirements should be checked against the official application instructions, especially when the preset may be stale.
